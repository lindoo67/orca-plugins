Modifications by Orca: JSON formatting; the $schema key is removed. In the HTML grammar, quoted attribute values and <script>/<style> bodies also include the JSP grammar's expression, EL and scriptlet rules, its `injections` (which flag stray `<` characters) are removed, and tags it does not recognise (JSP custom tags) are no longer marked invalid, because Orca does not read grammar injections.
